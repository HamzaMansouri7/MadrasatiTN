Madrasati TN is a **collaborative educational resource hub** where teachers share exercises, corrections, articles & book references with parents; AI enriches (summary, exercise generation, hints); parents discover & practice with their child. Teacher↔parent communication is scoped to resources/child learning.

**It is NOT:** online school, virtual classroom, schedule/lesson manager, video-course platform, or traditional LMS.

**Content model = Resource** (pivot away from "Course"):
- Exercise (question, correction, difficulty, subject, topic)
- Article (explanation, summary, references)
- Exercise Collection (grouped exercises)
- Book / Reference (author, chapters, topics, related exercises)

Key hierarchy: **Book → Chapter → Topic → Exercise → Correction**. AI sits across it (summarize / generate exercises from a chapter / explain+hints).

Flow: Teachers create & curate → AI enriches → Parents discover & practice → both communicate.

**How to apply:** parents seek exercices first — exercices default over books (already split in [[delete-exact-duplicates]]-organized library). Prefer "Resource" framing over "Course" in new features. Books link to exercises as references ("Based on Chapter 4 of X").
